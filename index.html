Pricing

Exclusive offer prices apply to new accounts in select Lumos markets. Taxes, fees and surcharges are extra. Downgrades from existing Lumos plans do not qualify for offer pricing.

Speeds

Speeds are “up to” the maximum advertised upload and download rate, measured on a wired connection. Actual speeds may vary by location and are not guaranteed.

Wi-Fi equipment

Wi-Fi is included on 100 Mbps – 8 Gig orders in select markets. Larger spaces may need additional extenders for complete coverage; additional charges may apply.

Prepaid Mastercard®

The $500 Lumos Prepaid Mastercard® applies to new accounts on qualifying 2 Gig, 5 Gig and 8 Gig plans in select markets. Offer may change or end without notice.

Availability

The 100% fiber-optic network is not available in all areas. Plan and offer availability depend on your service address.

Combining offers

Offers cannot be combined with any other promotion. Equipment and additional services beyond those listed are extra.
